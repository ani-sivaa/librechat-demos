# Demo 2: Your Personal AI Team

## What This Demo Does
Instead of having one generic AI assistant, this demo gives you a whole team of specialists. Each one is set up to be really good at a specific job - like having a coding expert, a creative writer, and a data analyst all ready to help you.

## What Makes This Cool
- Ready-to-use AI assistants that are already trained for specific jobs
- Each assistant has its own personality and way of talking
- Settings are already tuned to get the best results for each type of work
- Switch between different experts with just a click
- Search through all your old conversations
- Share your assistant setups with friends or teammates

## Meet Your AI Team
- **Python Developer** - Writes clean code and follows all the best practices
- **Creative Writer** - Great at stories, creative content, and imaginative writing
- **Data Analyst** - Helps you understand numbers and suggests charts and graphs
- **Code Reviewer** - Looks at your code and tells you how to make it better
- **Quick Drafts** - Perfect for brainstorming and getting ideas down fast

## Why You'd Want This
- Get help that's tailored to exactly what you're working on
- No need to explain what you want every single time
- Each assistant gives you the style of help that works best for that task
- Perfect if you're working on a team and want everyone to get similar responses

## How to Get Started
1. Copy the example settings: `cp .env.example .env`
2. Edit the `.env` file and add your API keys
3. Start everything up: `docker-compose up -d`
4. Open your browser and go to http://localhost:3081 (note the different port!)
5. Import the preset templates and start working with your AI team

## Where to Get Your Free Keys
- **Google Gemini**: Go to https://makersuite.google.com/app/apikey (completely free)
- **Groq**: Sign up at https://console.groq.com (free tier is generous)

## Ready-Made Assistant Templates
Look in the `presets/` folder for pre-built assistant configurations that you can import right into LibreChat. No setup required - just load them and start working!
